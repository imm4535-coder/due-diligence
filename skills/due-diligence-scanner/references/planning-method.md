# Plan investigations around decisions

The plan separates what is known, what might explain it, what evidence could distinguish explanations, and what action would change. Investigation depth is proportional to the stakes, reversibility, uncertainty, available access and time.

Start with plausible alternatives, including a benign explanation, an adverse explanation and “not enough evidence” where applicable. For causal questions distinguish symptoms, mechanisms, enabling conditions, incentives and interventions. Five Whys and a plausible narrative generate hypotheses; they do not establish causation. Require temporal ordering, competing explanations and evidence of the mechanism. A document's provenance and the causal origin of an event are different questions.

For future direction use conditional scenarios, assumptions, sensitivity/switching conditions and observable indicators. Avoid reading executives' minds or treating declared intent as delivery capability. Ask what a person can authorize, resource and change, and which historical actions can be verified.

Value of Information concerns expected improvement in a decision from new evidence. In this starter, high/medium/low judgments help prioritize; they are not a formal expected-value calculation. Defer optional tasks whose plausible results do not change an action, unless they serve a documented assurance purpose. Required legal/contractual/safety work remains required. If no data supports numeric probability, do not manufacture one.

## Reference foundations, with scope limits

- [ISO 31000](https://www.iso.org/standard/31000): risk principles and management in context.
- [IEC 31010](https://www.iso.org/standard/72140.html): selecting risk-assessment techniques for the situation.
- [OECD due diligence guidance](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-due-diligence-guidance-for-responsible-business-conduct_15f5f4b3-en.html): risk-based responsible business conduct; this does not replace financial/legal transaction diligence.
- [HM Treasury Magenta Book](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-magenta-book): theory of change, evaluation and examination of causal mechanisms/alternative explanations.
- [Value of Information research](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.08994): deciding which additional evidence is worth collecting.
- [NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework): governance and evaluation when AI is involved.

These references inform the method. No compliance certification or formal standards conformity assessment is claimed. Verify current rules and professional requirements for an actual jurisdiction during an authorized investigation.
