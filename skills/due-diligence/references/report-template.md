# Decision memo template

## Decision and scope
Subject / legal identity; decision; jurisdiction; review date; deadline; reviewer; selected domains; review depth; materiality; exclusions and limitations.

## Provisional recommendation
Hold / needs review / ready for human review, with rationale and conditions. Human decision owner: __. No automatic approval.

## Established observations
Claim | source ID and locator | as-of date | verification performed | confidence and limitations.

## Material risks and contradictions
Finding | impact / severity rationale | evidence and counter-evidence | unknowns | current state.

## Evidence coverage and confidence
Documentation coverage if calculated; denominator and N/A reasoning; source independence and verification limits; missing or stale documents. Never label coverage a safety score.

## Action plan
Action | owner | due date | evidence required to close | decision condition. Accepted risks retain a named accountable owner and rationale.

## Evidence register
ID | title | type | locator / page / URL | as-of / retrieval dates | excerpt | provenance | limitations.

## Residual risk and human decision
Required specialist reviews; conditions precedent; accountable decision owner; decision and rationale to be recorded by that person; next review date; triggers for reopening.
