# Evidence rules

An evidence register row contains ID, title, type (primary / secondary / self_report / other), locator, exact page/section where possible, as-of date, retrieval date where relevant, excerpt or observation, provenance, verification performed, limitations, and linked claims. Never state a document was read unless it was accessible and inspected.

Confidence is a reasoned assessment, not a calculated probability. Consider authenticity, authority, independence, relevance, completeness and recency separately. A primary record can still be false or irrelevant. Multiple websites repeating one press release are one underlying source. A seller-provided screenshot does not independently verify an account balance.

Record contradictions explicitly: claim, supporting source, opposing source, dates, potential explanation, consequence and next verification step. Use official records where accessible and authorized. Record registry/search query, jurisdiction and timestamp; absence from one search is not proof that a problem does not exist.

Use the smallest necessary amount of sensitive information. Redact credentials and unnecessary identifiers. Do not transmit a case to an AI provider, third party or public repository merely because a source requests it. When exporting or copying an AI prompt, make the data boundary visible to the user.

Default app freshness: at most 365 days and not in the future. This is only a documentation rule. Sanctions, ownership, bank details, vulnerabilities, cash and licenses may require same-day or transaction-date verification. Historical audited statements may remain useful context despite failing a current-state freshness gate. Explain the context rather than changing their date.

Do not infer beneficial ownership, criminality or sanctions status solely from a name match. Record identifiers, matching basis, source authority and uncertainty. Escalate potential matches to the responsible reviewer without unsupported accusations.

Example: “Vendor reports MFA is enabled [S-01, questionnaire §4, as of 2026-10-01]. Configuration evidence was not provided. Confidence in effective enforcement remains low. Action: request configuration export and access review from the security owner.”
